## About Me

I'm a **Master's student in Software Engineering (MESW)** at the **Faculty of Engineering, University of Porto (FEUP)**, with a background in Computer Engineering (BSc from UTAD, 2022–2025).

I'm also a researcher at **INESC TEC**, where my work sits at the intersection of machine learning and behavioral digital phenotyping, specifically applying ML techniques to mental health monitoring and depression severity discrimination.

I enjoy working across the full stack, from designing backend architectures and APIs to building clean, functional frontends. I'm drawn to problems that blend engineering rigor with real-world impact, whether that's in research, product, or tooling.
